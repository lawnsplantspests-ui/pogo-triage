How to shoot them — read this once

Shoot the box grid, not one Pokémon at a time. Because your nicknames carry the IVs (pika131214 = 13 attack, 12 defence, 14 HP), the grid view already has everything needed for a verdict. One screenshot of the list becomes a dozen rows here.

Scroll a screen, screenshot, scroll, screenshot. Then drop the whole batch in at once.

Single Pokémon screens work too and add the CP and moves. For anything not renamed, it falls back to reading the appraisal bars — so shoot the appraisal screen for those.

Legacy moves

Low CP, high bulk

The best Great League mons look like junk in a CP sort. Low attack buys bulk under the cap.

Shadows

Purple flame = 20% more damage than the clean version. A shadow bench mon outranks a normal one.
